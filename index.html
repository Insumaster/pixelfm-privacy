Privacy policy - Pixel Football Manager

Last updated: October 2026

Pixel Football Manager is a game you play on your own phone. This page says what happens with your data.

What we collect. Nothing. The game does not collect, send or sell any personal data. There is no account, no login, no ads and no tracking.

Your saves. Your careers are saved only on your phone, in the game's own storage. They are removed if you uninstall the game.

Internet. The game does not need the internet and does not talk to any server while you play.

Purchases. There are no in-app purchases. If a one-time Plus purchase comes later, it is handled by Google Play and the game only checks whether you own it.

Children. The game does not ask for any personal information and can be played by everyone.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be in the game under ? and on the store page.

Contact. Questions? Use the contact e-mail on the game's Google Play page.
